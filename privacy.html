How we protect your information

A plain-language promise to every family. Draft for the parish council to review.

  • The parish owns your dataNot a volunteer, not a company. The parish council is responsible for it.
  • Never sold or sharedYour details are used only for parish life and tax receipts.
  • People see only what they needA unit coordinator sees their own unit. Nobody but the accounts team sees giving.
  • Children are protectedParents manage children's details. Children never appear in the directory.
  • You stay in controlSee, correct or download your details any time, and ask us to remove them.
  • Questions?Contact the parish privacy contact (to be named by the council).
