# Holy Family Parish website: clickable prototype

A clickable prototype of the new website for Holy Family Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Halifax, Nova Scotia. It was made for the parish council meeting in November 2026.

**This is not the real website.** All families, names, numbers and events are samples. Buttons show a short message about what the real website would do. Nothing is saved.

## Demo sign-in

- Email: `demo@holyfamilyhalifax.ca`
- Password: `HolyFamily2026`

The sign-in only works in this browser and protects nothing. It just opens the sample member pages.

## What to look at

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Home | The next Holy Mass (worked out from the weekly schedule), quick links, news, events, family units |
| Mass times | Weekly schedule, confession, sacrament requests |
| Events | Events with calendar, registration and WhatsApp sharing |
| Community | Associations and the six family units, with "find my unit" |
| Register your family | The 4-step registration flow |
| My account | Family dashboard after sign-in |
| My family | Family members, sacraments, contact details |
| Tax receipts | Download receipts by year |
| My giving | Monthly tithe and special causes (later phase) |
| Parish directory | Members-only, opt-in, names and units only |
| Privacy and settings | Directory choice, messages, download or close account |
| Parish office | The view for the priest, trustees and approved volunteers |
